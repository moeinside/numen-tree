# numen.fight

> Combat: attack one entity you name. numen.fight.clear (library) fights off every hostile near you, one by one.

| 函数 | 签名 | 摘要 | 原子 | 树角色 | 优先级 | 实测 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `numen.fight.attack` | `fun(entity: integer\|Entity): Fought Attack one entity until it is dead, lost or out of reach.` | Attack one entity until it is dead, lost or out of reach. | ❓ | ❓ | ❓ | ❓ |
| `numen.fight.clear` | `fun(radius?: integer): integer Attack every hostile mob within radius blocks of you, nearest first, one numen.fight.attack each, until none is left.` |  | ❓ | ❓ | ❓ | ⚠️ |
