---Your inventory: what you carry, recipes, crafting, eating, dropping.
---@class numen.inv
---@field count fun(item: string): integer How many of one item you carry in your backpack.
---@field craft fun(recipe: string, opts?: {count?: integer}): Crafted Craft once in the crafting grid you have open: lay one recipe into it and take the result.
---@field craftable fun(): Recipe[] Every crafting recipe you can craft right now from what you carry.
---@field drop fun(item: string, opts?: {count?: integer}): Dropped Drop items from your inventory on the ground in front of you.
---@field eat fun(item: string): Ate Eat or drink something from your inventory.
---@field items fun(): Stack[] What you carry in your backpack, one entry per kind of item.
---@field recipes fun(item: string): Recipe[] How an item is made, like JEI: every recipe that outputs it, at every station.
---@field make fun(item: string, count?: integer): integer Make count of item (default 1): pick a crafting recipe you have the materials for (numen.inv.recipes), and craft it with numen.inv.craft until there are enough.
---@field store fun(at: Pos|Block, item: string, count?: integer): integer Walk to a container (a chest, a barrel …), open it, put count of item in (all you carry by default) and close it.
---@field fetch fun(at: Pos|Block, item: string, count?: integer): integer Walk to a container, open it, take count of item out (all it holds by default) and close it.
---@field smelt fun(furnace: Pos|Block, item: string, count?: integer, opts?: {fuel?: string, timeout?: number}): integer Smelt count of item (all you carry by default) in a furnace, blast furnace or smoker: walk to it, put the item in and opts.fuel (default minecraft:coal, one per 8 items), stand by until the input is used up and nothing is cooking (numen.time.wait_until, opts.timeout seconds, default 12 per item), take every output out and close it.
---@field give fun(target: Entity|Pos, item: string, count?: integer): integer Give count of item (all you carry by default) to a player or another entity: walk within 2 blocks of them (numen.move.to with arrive "near") and drop it there for them to pick up.
numen.inv = {}

---What one craft made.
---@class Crafted
---@field crafted integer How many it made.
---@field carrying integer How many you carry now.
---@field used string[] What it used, 3x oak_planks.
---@field got_back string[] What came back, a bucket from milk.
---@field more boolean Whether what you carry crafts more of it: call again for the rest.

---One way to make an item, like a page of JEI.
---@class Recipe
---@field id string The recipe's id; numen.inv.craft takes it.
---@field station "crafting"|"smelting"|"blasting"|"smoking"|"campfire"|"stonecutter"|"smithing" Where it is made.
---@field item string What it makes.
---@field makes integer How many one craft makes.
---@field ingredients string[] One entry per filled cell: an item, or planks(any) for any of a kind.
---@field grid? integer Crafting only: the smallest grid it fits, 2 (your own) or 3 (a crafting table); 0 needs a modded station.
---@field missing? string[] Crafting only: what you are short of to craft it once, 3x iron_ingot (have 1); absent when you have it all.
---@field ticks? integer Cooking only: how long one item takes.

---@class Dropped
---@field item string
---@field dropped integer
---@field remaining_in_inventory integer

---@class Ate
---@field item string
---@field hp number
---@field hunger integer

---@class Stack
---@field item string The item id, minecraft:cobblestone.
---@field count integer How many you carry, all stacks together.
