---Combat: attack one entity you name. numen.fight.clear (library) fights off every hostile near you, one by one.
---@class numen.fight
---@field attack fun(entity: integer|Entity): Fought Attack one entity until it is dead, lost or out of reach.
---@field clear fun(radius?: integer): integer Attack every hostile mob within radius blocks of you, nearest first, one numen.fight.attack each, until none is left.
numen.fight = {}

---What an attack did: each entity it fought and how that ended.
---@class Fought
---@field fought Foe[] Each entity it fought, the one you named first.
---@field strikes integer Hits in all.

---One entity an attack fought.
---@class Foe
---@field id integer Its runtime id.
---@field status string defeated, lost, unreachable, refused: <why>, or pending.
---@field strikes integer Hits it took from you.
