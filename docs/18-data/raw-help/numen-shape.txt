---Positions and shapes: Pos values that add and measure, shapes drawn as Cells, and Cells you can turn, move and combine.
---@class numen.shape
---@field pos fun(x: number, y: number, z: number): Pos A Pos at x, y, z.
---@field cells fun(list: (Block|Pos)[]): Cells Cells from a list of Blocks (a block to put in each cell) or Pos (just the cells): a scan's blocks, numen.build.diff's result, your own list.
---@field box fun(from: Pos, to: Pos, block?: string, hollow?: boolean): Cells Every cell of the box between two corners, or only its faces when hollow.
---@field line fun(from: Pos, to: Pos, block?: string): Cells A line of cells from one point to another, diagonals included: beams, posts, ridges.
---@field cylinder fun(base: Pos, radius: integer, height: integer, block?: string, hollow?: boolean): Cells A cylinder standing on its bottom centre: towers, wells, round rooms; only its wall when hollow.
---@field sphere fun(center: Pos, radius: integer, block?: string, hollow?: boolean): Cells A sphere around its centre: domes, globes; only its shell when hollow.
---@field layer fun(at: Pos, rows: string[], legend: table<string, string>): Cells One level drawn as a character grid: a floor, a wall ring, a roof course, a window pattern.
