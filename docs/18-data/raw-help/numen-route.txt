---Preference knobs for one walk; leave out what you don't care about.
---@class Costs
---@field dig? boolean|number false: never break a block (default). true: may dig through, each block costing its dig time plus 30. A number: may dig, with that extra cost per block.
---@field place? boolean|number false: never place a block (default). true: may pillar and bridge with materials, 20 per block. A number: may, with that cost per block.
---@field consent? boolean|number Cells your owner must agree to change (their builds, their chests …). A number (default 10): plannable at that many times the price; the plan lists them and the walk stops at each to ask. false: keep away from them, never bother your owner.
---@field jump? number Extra cost per jump (default 2); raise it for a flatter walk.
---@field swim? number Extra cost per block through water (default 3).
---@field fall? integer Highest drop to take without water below (default 3); higher only when your health can take it.
---@field parkour? boolean Allow running jumps over 2-4 block gaps (default false).
---@field max_changes? integer How many blocks the whole walk may break and place at most; ways over it are dropped (default: no limit).

---A walk planned from where you stood, without moving: what numen.move.go walks and keeps to. Good only within the program that made it.
---@class Plan
---@field ok boolean No leg is unreachable: numen.move.go can walk it.
---@field why? string When not ok: why the unreachable leg can't be walked.
---@field id string Which plan this is, for numen.move.go.
---@field spec Directions The description you gave, as given: change it and plan again.
---@field from Pos Where it was planned from.
---@field steps integer Steps of the seen part, all legs.
---@field seconds number About how long the seen part takes.
---@field legs Leg[] One per stop, the destination last.
