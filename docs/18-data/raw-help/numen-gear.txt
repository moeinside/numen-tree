---What you wear and hold: putting it on, taking it in hand, taking it off.
---@class numen.gear
---@field hold fun(item: string, opts?: {hand?: "main"|"off"}): Change Take an item from your backpack into your hand.
---@field remove fun(opts?: {slot?: string, item?: string}): Change Take gear off back into your backpack.
---@field wear fun(item: string, opts?: {slot?: string}): Change Wear an item from your backpack: armor, or an accessory a mod adds slots for.

---@class Change
