---Find the nearest structure or biome in the dimension you are in.
---@class numen.locate
---@field biome fun(biome: string): Located Find the nearest biome of a type: its column, compass direction and distance.
---@field structure fun(structure: string): Located Find the nearest structure of a type: its column, compass direction and distance.
numen.locate = {}

---Where the nearest one is, or how far the search went without finding one.
---@class Located
---@field found boolean Whether one was found.
---@field pos? Pos|{x: number, z: number}|{y: number} The column it is in: numen.move.to takes it and finds the height on its own.
---@field direction? string Compass direction from where you stand.
---@field horizontal_distance? integer Blocks to it, horizontally.
---@field searched? integer How far out the search went when nothing was found; 0 means it does not generate in this dimension.
---@field dimension string The dimension searched: yours.
